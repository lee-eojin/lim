# Sources and licenses

Read this when the user asks where a rule comes from. Strength of support for each Korean rule is in `korean-rules.md`.

## Licensing approach

- **ASD-STE100 is the basis, and it is not reproduced.** ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English (Issue 9, 2025) is a published standard whose text and controlled dictionary are copyrighted by its maintainer, with no open license. This skill summarizes its principles in its own words (one meaning per word, short sentences, active voice, one instruction per sentence, conditions first, noun chains of three or fewer) and quotes none of its rules or dictionary entries. Word examples come from public-domain plain-language lists. This skill is not an STE compliance tool, and the STE maintenance group does not recognize compliance claims made for tools.
- **Copied with adaptation:** the U.S. federal Plain Language word list, which is in the public domain and also published under CC0.
- **Summarized in new words, with links:** Korean public-language guidance whose license allows non-commercial use without changes, and developer documentation style guides.
- **Korean government material** under the Korea Open Government License type 1 (attribution) may be reused; the word lists in `korean-rules.md` section 8 draw on it.

## Korean plain language

- National Institute of Korean Language (2019), plain public language guide (Korea Open Government License type 1): https://www.korean.go.kr/front/etcData/etcDataView.do?mn_id=208&etc_seq=647
- National Institute of Korean Language (2021), guideline for plain administrative language (Korea Open Government License type 4, non-commercial, no changes; summarized only): https://www.korean.go.kr/front/reportData/reportDataView.do?mn_id=207&report_seq=1122
- National Institute of Korean Language, public question-and-answer service on grammar (the basis for the over-correction notes): https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaList.do?mn_id=216
- Ministry of Government Legislation, standards for plain legal language (10th edition, 2026): scope of 및, 또는, 등; pointing words; double negatives; double passives; dropped subjects; and the rule not to force-translate technical terms. https://www.moleg.go.kr/
- Framework Act on the Korean Language, Article 14 (public documents in plain language): https://www.law.go.kr/
